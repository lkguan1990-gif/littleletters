Little Letters – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

The short version

  • Little Letters has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • What you choose in the app (letters, theme, notes, settings) stays on your device.
  • I do not collect your name, email address, location, photos or anything about your child.
  • If you buy the unlock, the app store and my purchase provider handle the payment and confirm the purchase. That is the only data that leaves the device.

Who I am

Little Letters is made by Kian Guan Lee, an independent developer based in Singapore. I am the person responsible for this app and this policy. You can reach me at lohkianshan@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

Little Letters is a companion for young children who are learning to write on paper, and it is meant to be set up and used together with a parent or carer. The settings, the unlock and the About page are for the grown-up, and the purchase sits behind a question that is meant for adults. The app does not ask a child for any information.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your phone or tablet so that it remembers your choices next time:

  • the letters you picked and the letters chosen for focus mode;
  • your theme, help, voice and light or dark mode settings;
  • any notes you type about a letter or a step;
  • which theme unlock you have, so the app can keep it open.

This information is not sent to me or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. The spoken instructions are recordings that are part of the app itself, and the app does not use your microphone or camera.

What I do not collect

I do not collect, and the app does not ask for: your name, email address, phone number, address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, or your child's name, age or voice. I do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and I do not sell or share personal information.

Purchases

The app is free to use with the Cars and Horse themes. You can choose to buy a one-time unlock for all themes. If you do:

  • The payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I never see your card or payment details.
  • I use a purchase service, RevenueCat, to check that a purchase is genuine and to restore it if you reinstall the app or change device. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random, anonymous identifier created for your installation. It is not linked to your name or email address by me. How RevenueCat handles that data is described in its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing about your child for me to store, use or share. If you believe a child has somehow given me personal information, please write to me and I will delete it.

Your choices

  • You can delete everything the app has saved at any time by deleting the app.
  • If you want to ask about, or have removed, any data connected to a purchase, write to me at the address above, and I will help with what I can. Purchase records are also kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the effective date above, and I will tell you in the app's update notes where the change is significant.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Write to lohkianshan@gmail.com.
